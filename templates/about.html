About Smart Farm Assistant

Smart Farm Assistant is an IoT-powered smart crop protection system designed to help farmers monitor and protect their agricultural fields.

Our Mission

Our goal is to use Artificial Intelligence, IoT, cameras and automation to help farmers protect crops from animals and improve farm security.

How It Works

A camera continuously monitors the agricultural field. The system uses YOLO AI to detect animals such as dogs, cats, cows, elephants and other supported animals.

When an animal is detected, the system can automatically send a signal to Arduino, capture an image and show notifications to the user.
